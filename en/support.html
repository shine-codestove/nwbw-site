Frequently Asked Questions

How do I import a GPX file?

Tap a GPX file on your iPhone and an "Open in NWBW" option appears. You can open GPX files from the Files app, email, web downloads, and more. Imported courses show up in the iPhone app, where you can sync them to your Apple Watch.

How do I choose a course on Apple Watch?

First, sync the course from your iPhone to your Watch. Then open the NWBW app on your Apple Watch to see the list of synced courses. Choose the course you want and tap "Start Run."

How will I know if I go off course?

If you stray more than 5 m from the course, your Watch shows a warning along with haptic feedback. The Navigation screen then guides you back toward the route.

Can I run with just my Watch?

Yes. Once a course is synced, you can run and navigate with your Apple Watch alone, without your iPhone. GPS, heart rate, distance, and all other data are recorded on the Watch.

What is Companion Mode?

When you run with your iPhone, this mode uses the iPhone's GPS to save your Watch's battery. The iPhone handles GPS tracking and navigation calculations, while the Watch focuses on displaying your workout data.

Why does the app need location permission?

GPS location is needed to record your running route and provide navigation. Choosing "Always Allow" keeps recording accurate even while the screen is off. Location data is stored only on your devices and is never sent anywhere else.

Why does the app need Health (HealthKit) permission?

Heart rate, distance, and calorie measurements and workout saving all go through the Apple Health app, so recording a run requires Health permission. You only need to approve it once; you won't be asked again. If you denied permission, you'll see a notice when you start a run, and you can change it at any time on your Watch under Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

I approved Health permission, but I keep getting prompted

This was a known issue in earlier versions and has been fixed in the latest version. Please update NWBW to the latest version on the App Store. After updating, the app quietly checks permissions you've already granted and won't ask again.

How does climb guidance work?

When you start a course run, the app analyzes the GPX elevation data to identify climbs ahead of time. About 200 m before a climb begins, a voice prompt tells you its length, average grade, and elevation gain, and the Elevation screen shows the real-time grade and the gain left.

How do I add race checkpoints and cutoffs?

In the iPhone app, open Course Details → Race CPs and add each CP's name, distance from the start, aid station, and cutoff (time since the start). Saving sends the course to your Watch again, and the course card shows when it's saved there. If you haven't added CPs, waypoints (<wpt>) in the GPX are used as CPs automatically. During the run, the Checkpoints screen shows distance to the next CP, estimated arrival, and time to cutoff, with voice alerts 500 m ahead and as you pass.

Which guidance level should I choose?

Choose it in Settings → Running → Guidance level. Minimal suits well-marked races (one alert when you're clearly off course), Normal suits everyday trails, and Precise suits unfamiliar routes (major forks announced 60 m ahead).

What happens to my run if the app closes or the battery dies?

Your run is saved as you go. If the app closes, reopen it to return to the run screen and keep going. If the battery dies, the app asks whether to save that run the next time you open it. You'll also get alerts at 20% and 10% battery, and GPS switches to battery saver at 10%.

When should I use Backtrack?

If you're lost or need to turn back partway, tap "Backtrack" on the Controls screen. It guides you in reverse along the route you've run so far, so you can return safely to the start. Your workout keeps recording the whole time.

Where do I turn on Auto-Pause and Target Pace alerts?

You can turn them on in the iPhone app under Settings > Run Assist. Auto-Pause stops recording automatically when you stop and resumes when you start moving again, and Target Pace alerts you by voice when you're running more than 20% slower than the pace you set. Settings sync to your Watch automatically.

How much battery does it use?

Because GPS and the display are in constant use, it uses more battery than a typical app. On Apple Watch Ultra, you can expect about 8–10 hours of continuous use. Companion Mode can save 30–40% of your Watch's battery.

How do I record laps with the Action Button (Apple Watch Ultra)?

On your Watch, go to Settings → Action Button → Workout → App and select NWBW. Press the Action Button during a run to record a lap, confirmed with a haptic tap. Press it before a run to start a Free Run instantly. On watches without an Action Button, use the Mark Lap button at the bottom of the Laps screen.

I finished a run, but it doesn't appear in the Records tab on my iPhone

With your Watch and iPhone connected, open the iPhone app once. The Watch automatically resends any records your iPhone is missing. You can also find the same records on the Run History card in the Watch app.

Is heart rate data uploaded to Strava too?

Yes. Starting with version 0.3.6, the Watch records your heart rate every 5 seconds and includes it in Strava uploads and shared GPX files. For runs recorded after the update, you can see your heart rate graph and average heart rate on Strava.

Can I use the app in English?

Yes. Starting with version 0.3.7, the entire app is available in English. In the iPhone app, go to Settings → General → Language and choose System, 한국어, or English. Your Watch follows the iPhone's setting and switches to the same language; if you're in the middle of a run, the change applies once the run ends. System follows your device language, and if your device language is neither Korean nor English, the app is shown in English.

Can I send feedback from the app?

Yes. In the iPhone app, go to Settings → About → Send Feedback to open an email to info@codestove.io. To help us look into issues quickly, the app version, device model, and iOS version are added automatically at the end of the email. You can delete them before sending if you prefer.

Can I support the developer?

You can, with "Buy the Developer an Energy Gel" at the bottom of the Settings tab in the iPhone app. It's an optional in-app purchase of one energy gel at a time ($2.99 in the US, ₩3,000 in Korea), processed by Apple. Every NWBW feature is free and works the same whether or not you donate.

Tips

📂 Finding GPX Files

You can download GPX files from Strava, AllTrails, and trail race websites.

🔋 Saving Battery

For long courses, use Companion Mode or check your Watch screen less often to make the battery last longer.

📍 GPS Accuracy

Wait outdoors for about 30 seconds before starting your run to let the GPS signal settle.

⌚ Checking the Screen

Raise your wrist and the screen turns on automatically. Swipe to switch between the six views (Navigation, Map, Elevation, Stats, Laps, Controls).
